About Senior Social

A community-first platform for elderly companionship.

Senior Social India is created with one purpose: to reduce loneliness and make social connection easier for older adults. Whether someone wants to attend group activities, meet like-minded people, learn something new or simply have a trusted community to be part of, Senior Social provides a welcoming starting point.

Safety & Trust

Designed with care, clarity and confidence.

Senior Social India focuses on a safe, respectful and easy-to-use experience. The platform is designed to be simple for seniors while also giving families confidence that their loved ones can participate in meaningful social activities.

Member Verification

We review members before connecting them.

To keep Senior Social India safe, we treat every joining request as an interest registration first. Personal details are reviewed before any member is connected with groups, activities or other people.

Aadhaar safety note: We do not ask users to enter or submit their Aadhaar number on this website. If Aadhaar is used later, it should be voluntary and handled through authorised/offline verification methods such as Aadhaar offline QR verification, without publicly displaying personal details.

Verification options

📱Mobile verificationConfirm the member through their phone number.
✉️Email verificationConfirm the member using the email they provide.
👨‍👩‍👧Family contactOptionally confirm through a trusted family or emergency contact.
🪪Voluntary ID verificationAadhaar offline QR or other ID may be used later where appropriate.
